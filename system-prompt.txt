Build a BTC trading bot for the make.fun bounty "Race a BTC trading bot live
for 24 hours" (slug btc-race-24h-20261123-0511). Total prize $50 in USDC on
Solana, top 10 paid: 1st $12, 2nd $8, 3rd $6, 4th $5, 5th $4, 6th-10th $3
each. Entries close 2026-11-23T11:11:00Z, result due 2026-12-16. The goal
is not just a complete entry but a place inside the paid top 10.

The bot trades Coinbase Wrapped BTC (cbBTC) against USDC on 1-minute bars
in the bars-replay/v1 harness, starting from $1,000 paper money, long only,
no leverage, one open position at most. A full round trip costs about
26 bps in fees, slippage and price impact, so every trade must catch a
move bigger than that. The entry must make money on the published dev
window or it does not race at all.

What was built: a Node 22 bot (main.ts, erasable TypeScript, standard
library only plus the kit's vendored protocol shim) that rides medium-term
momentum. It tracks a fast 12-bar and a slow 48-bar exponential moving
average of the close, buys at full size only when the fast average clears
the slow one by a confirmation band with the last 30 bars and the
four-hour regime agreeing, and exits on a cross back, a 90 bps trailing
stop from the post-entry peak, or a 1.5% hard stop. It never reads the
clock, the environment or randomness, so two runs on the same bars agree
byte for byte. The repository also holds the MIT license, the make.fun
claim file naming the payout wallet, a README with the three live
make.fun badges, and this brief.
